## How to think about it
1. Set a budget: decide whether you need a full uniform or just key pieces. 2. Choose the series that resonates most with you—original, TNG, Discovery, etc. 3. Verify size charts and read customer reviews for fit accuracy. 4. Prioritize licensed accessories (phasers, badges) for the best look. 5. Consider long‑term use: pick fabrics that breathe and can survive multiple conventions.

<Callout type="warning">Avoid pointing replica phasers at people and never wear loose accessories in crowded spaces where they could cause tripping hazards.</Callout>
