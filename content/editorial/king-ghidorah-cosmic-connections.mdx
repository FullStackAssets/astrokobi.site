## What happened
Godzilla Minus Zero opened on November 6, 2026 and pits the iconic lizard against King Ghidorah, also called Monster Zero. In the film, Ghidorah is portrayed as a golden, three‑headed dragon that fell to Earth inside a highly magnetic meteorite, a nod to its original 1964 debut where it ravaged Venus before arriving on our planet.

Separately, the astronomical community has designated a massive galaxy supercluster the King Ghidorah Supercluster. This structure ranks among the largest known concentrations of galaxies, and its informal name was chosen to honor the cultural impact of the kaiju, not because of any physical connection.

## Why it matters
The dual presence of King Ghidorah in cinema and in the scientific lexicon illustrates how popular media can influence the language of science. When a monster’s name appears on a map of the universe, it sparks curiosity that can draw non‑specialists toward astrophysics. At the same time, the naming choice raises questions about the balance between engaging the public and maintaining scientific gravitas.

## How to think about it
