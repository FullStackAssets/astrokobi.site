The Star Trek community mourned the loss of Walter Koenig, the actor who brought Ensign Pavel Chekov to life, as the franchise marked its 60th anniversary. On Oct. 25, 2026, Koenig passed away at age 90, prompting an outpouring of grief from fans and fellow cast members. Two of the last surviving original‑series crew, William Shatner (95) and George Takei (89), posted heartfelt Instagram messages honoring their friend and colleague. Their tributes highlight the personal bonds that formed behind the iconic bridge of the USS Enterprise. Understanding how these gestures resonate reveals the cultural weight of long‑running science‑fiction sagas.

## What happened
William Shatner shared a somber Instagram post, saying he was “deeply saddened” by Koenig’s passing and recalling the actor’s genuine love for the Star Trek community and his willingness to engage with fans at conventions.

George Takei also posted on Instagram, describing his “heavy heart” and noting that Koenig had stood beside him as best man at his 2008 wedding. Takei remembered Koenig’s birthday on Sept. 14, the same day Takei and his partner celebrated their 18th anniversary, and praised Koenig as a writer, teacher, and loyal friend.
