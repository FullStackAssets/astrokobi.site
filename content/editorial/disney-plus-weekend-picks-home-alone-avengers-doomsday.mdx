Disney+ has rolled out a concise weekend lineup that blends holiday nostalgia, superhero excitement, and high‑fantasy adventure. The service is promoting the 1990 comedy Home Alone, a yet‑unreleased Marvel MCU entry titled Avengers: Doomsday, and a fantasy epic adapted from a bestselling novel starring Alexandra Daddario. These choices aim to keep subscribers entertained while priming them for the next wave of Disney+ originals and theatrical releases. With the holiday season kicking in, the timing aligns with increased family viewing. The mix also reflects Disney’s strategy of using familiar titles to drive platform engagement.

## What happened
On the weekend of November 14, Disney+ highlighted Home Alone (1990) as its top holiday‑season recommendation, noting its enduring appeal and the opportunity for families to revisit Kevin McCallister’s antics.

The platform also spotlighted Avengers: Doomsday, described as one of the biggest MCU films slated to precede the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday theatrical event, giving subscribers a preview of the tone and stakes.

Finally, Disney+ promoted a fantasy epic featuring Alexandra Daddario, based on a bestselling book that has been adapted twice, offering a high‑budget adventure for viewers seeking something beyond the familiar animated catalog.

## Why it matters
By pairing a beloved Christmas comedy with a major Marvel teaser and a high‑concept fantasy, Disney+ taps into three distinct audience segments: families gathering for the holidays, superhero fans tracking the MCU’s narrative arc, and viewers craving cinematic spectacle. The strategy drives watch time during a peak period, reinforces brand loyalty, and builds anticipation for upcoming theatrical releases that will later return to the streaming library.
