The Houston Rockets are scheduled to play the Phoenix Suns in a preseason matchup that will see former Rocket Kevin Durant return to his old home court. The game, set for early November, is being billed as a reunion for Durant, who spent his early NBA years with Houston before moving on to other franchises. Fans and analysts alike are watching to see how the former star meshes with his old teammates and the current Suns roster. This encounter could shape narratives about Durant's legacy and the Rockets' rebuilding strategy.

## What happened
The Rockets announced their travel itinerary to Phoenix, confirming the preseason contest will take place at the Footprint Center. Durant, now a key player for the Suns, confirmed his participation, noting the personal significance of returning to the arena where he began his NBA career.

The matchup will feature several young Rockets prospects alongside veteran players, providing a platform for the team to evaluate talent against a high‑profile opponent. Media coverage highlights the emotional narrative of Durant revisiting the city that launched his professional journey.

The game serves as a litmus test for the Rockets' development plans and offers Durant a stage to showcase his evolution since leaving Houston. A strong performance could boost the Suns' confidence heading into the regular season, while the Rockets can gauge how their emerging roster competes against elite talent.
