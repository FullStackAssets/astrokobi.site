Nebula’s travel‑competition series Jet Lag: The Game is taking its 20th season out of the living room and onto the big screen. The 90‑minute premiere of “Hide + Seek: Italy” will be screened on Dec 5 in more than 160 theatres across the United States, United Kingdom and Canada. Tickets go on sale Nov 5, giving fans a chance to experience the six‑day hide‑and‑seek challenge in Italy together. This marks the series’ largest international theatrical rollout, expanding a niche streaming format into mainstream cinema.

<Callout type="takeaway">Season 20 turns a streaming travel game into a coordinated theatrical event, broadening its audience.</Callout>

## What happened
Nebula partnered with Regal, Picturehouse, Cineworld and Landmark Cinemas to bring the 90‑minute “Hide + Seek: Italy” premiere to over 160 screens in three countries on Dec 5. The event follows the Season 19 “Japanorama” premiere in August and the subsequent international rollout of the Season 19 finale in October, which were the series’ first limited theatrical screenings.

The new season pits the three hosts—Sam Denby, Ben Doyle and Adam Chase—in a six‑day expert‑mode hide‑and‑seek across Italy’s coastal towns, mountain villages and Roman hills, removing the most powerful questions and adding new mechanics to raise the stakes.

## Why it matters
By moving a Nebula‑exclusive series into cinemas, the producers test a hybrid distribution model that blends streaming‑first content with traditional theatrical exposure. The rollout could attract non‑subscribers, boost brand visibility, and create new revenue streams through ticket sales and on‑screen trivia experiences. It also signals a broader trend of niche digital creators leveraging cinema to deepen fan engagement and generate buzz beyond platform algorithms.

## How to think about it
Treat the theatrical premiere as a marketing funnel: use the event to generate excitement, collect on‑site data (e.g., email sign‑ups for Nebula), and then funnel viewers back to the streaming platform for the full season. Pair the cinema experience with exclusive on‑screen trivia or QR codes that unlock bonus content online, reinforcing the hybrid model. Evaluate success not just by ticket revenue but by subsequent subscriber growth and social‑media engagement.
