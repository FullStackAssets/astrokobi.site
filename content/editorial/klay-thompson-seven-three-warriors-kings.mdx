## What happened
Thompson finished the contest with 23 points on an 8‑of‑15 field‑goal effort, including seven of his nine attempts from beyond the arc. He also recorded two rebounds, four assists, and two blocked shots, contributing across the stat sheet.

The Warriors leveraged his hot hand to pull ahead in the second half, outscoring the Kings during a decisive stretch that turned a close contest into a comfortable lead. Sacramento struggled to contain the barrage of threes, forcing the Warriors to rely heavily on perimeter scoring.
