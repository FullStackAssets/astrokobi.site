The Paramount+ Black Friday promotion has cut the monthly price to just $2.99 for the first two months, giving fans instant access to nearly every Star Trek series and movie. The deal arrives as the franchise celebrates its 60th anniversary, with the latest season of Strange New Worlds wrapping up in September. For Trekkies and casual viewers alike, the offer is a low‑cost gateway to a decades‑long legacy. It also underscores how streaming services use nostalgia to drive subscriber growth. The promotion ends on November 30, after which the price reverts to the standard rates.

<Callout type="takeaway">For two months, Paramount+ offers full Star Trek access for $2.99/month, a rare price point that could reshape streaming habits.</Callout>

## What happened
Paramount+ announced the $2.99/month Black Friday deal on November 18, 2026, targeting both the new Strange New Worlds and the original series. The promotion covers the Essential and Premium tiers, with ad‑supported and ad‑free options respectively. Subscribers can cancel at any time, and the offer expires on November 30.

The discount aligns with the 60th anniversary of the first Star Trek episode, 'The Man Trap,' aired in 1966. Paramount+ hosts 12 original series and 14 movies, including the new Starfleet Academy and the recent season four of Strange New Worlds. The deal is part of a broader strategy to attract both long‑time fans and new audiences.

After the two‑month period, the Essential plan returns to $8.99/month and the Premium plan to $13.99/month. The promotion is limited to the first two months only, and users can cancel anytime.

## Why it matters
The offer demonstrates how legacy franchises can generate revenue spikes during holiday shopping periods. It also highlights the tension between low‑price access and the sustainability of streaming budgets. For Paramount+, the promotion could boost short‑term subscriber numbers, but the subsequent price hike may deter retention. Fans benefit from a cost‑effective way to explore the franchise, but the limited window may pressure them into quick decisions.

## How to think about it
When evaluating such deals, consider the total cost of ownership over the subscription period, the content you actually plan to watch, and whether the ad‑supported tier meets your tolerance for interruptions. Compare the discounted rate to the standard monthly price to calculate the break‑even point. Also, review the cancellation policy to ensure you can exit without penalty if the content mix changes.

<Callout type="warning">If you plan to keep the subscription beyond the two‑month window, be prepared for the price jump to $8.99 or $13.99 and factor that into your budget.</Callout>
